Strategic risks I would manage in every deal

Regulatory uncertainty: Voice AI consent and likeness laws are evolving. I pre-empt this by involving legal early and framing ElevenLabs' safety framework as risk mitigation, not a feature. Budget routing: CIOs bucket voice AI as a $30K departmental tool instead of $500K platform infrastructure. I reframe the buying conversation before it starts. Brand/IP risk: Synthetic voice in customer-facing channels triggers brand safety concerns. I surface this objection proactively and use ElevenLabs' consent verification and safety framework to resolve it in the security review, not the boardroom.

Territory Strategy & Pipeline Generation

How I Would Build a $2M+ Enterprise Book

Three Wedges. Specific Buyers. Specific Triggers.

Wedge 1: Contact Center Voice Agents

Who: VP CX + CIO at financial services, healthcare and telecom with 500+ agent seats. Trigger: Missed CSAT targets, rising AHT, RFPs for contact center modernization. Math: 500-seat center at $15/hr loaded cost = $15.6M/yr. If agents handle 40% of volume at $0.08/min, spend is ~$840K against $6.2M in labor offset. I would present this as a CFO-ready TCO model, not a feature deck.

Wedge 2: Localization for Global Product Teams

Who: VP Marketing + Head of Localization at SaaS companies in 10+ markets. Trigger: International expansion mandates, content velocity gaps. Math: Traditional dubbing costs $150-$300/min. ElevenLabs reduces this by 90%+. For a company localizing 100 hours of content across 10 languages, that is $1M+ in savings and weeks of time recovered.

Wedge 3: Voice Layer for Existing AI Agent Stacks

Who: CTO + Head of AI at enterprises already building agents on Salesforce, ServiceNow or custom stacks. Trigger: Agent initiative funded but text-only. Voice is the missing layer. Entry: This is an API-led sale. Land with one workflow, prove latency and quality, then expand across all agent touchpoints.

Deal Inspection Standards I Would Apply

Qualification
No deal enters pipeline without: a confirmed use case, a named champion, an identified economic buyer and a procurement timeline. If I cannot verify these, it is not a deal.
Forecasting
Commit = PO expected this quarter with confirmed signature path. Best case = all stakeholders aligned but procurement timeline uncertain. Nothing else enters the forecast.
Multi-Threading
Stage 3+ requires 3+ validated contacts across different functions. Single-thread deals get flagged in pipeline review and either get multi-threaded or downstaged.
Expansion Seeding
Before any deal closes, the expansion conversation is started: additional languages, additional queues, internal agent use cases. Land is not the goal. Platform adoption is.
